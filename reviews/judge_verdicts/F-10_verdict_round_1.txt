[ADVISORY #1] _live_contracts is not date-aware for rolled-out contracts
  File: src/finance/leverage.py:603–620
  Impact: Flagged by Actor as a pre-existing latent bug (not F-10). Confirmed:
    rolled_out = {event.old_contract for event in ledger.roll_events}
  The old_contract is excluded from ALL dates once any roll occurs, including
  dates before the roll was executed. In `compute_leaps_nav_contribution` and
  `compute_leaps_mtm` (used by external callers querying historical positions)
  this can silently under-count MTM before the roll date. Within run_backtest's
  own forward-only loop the bug is benign (the query date is always >= the roll
  date by construction), but downstream analysis functions are affected.
  Recommendation: Track (contract, roll_date) pairs and filter by
  current_date >= roll_date. File a follow-up issue; do NOT block F-10 on it.
