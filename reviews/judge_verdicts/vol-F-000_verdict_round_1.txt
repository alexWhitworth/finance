WHY FAIL (single blocking defect)
---------------------------------
The F-0 acceptance scope is "Carry wiring" — r(T) maturity-matched carry plus the
MOVE conversion fix. The conversion fix is fully implemented and verified. The
carry wiring delivers `fetch_2yr_treasury` (6d1125e, pre-existing) and the pure
`risk_free_at_expiry`, but **no production caller composes them**: risk_free_at_
expiry is exported (src/finance/data.py:336) and referenced only by tests. No
composition reads ^IRX + fetch_2yr_treasury and feeds risk_free_at_expiry, and the
spec's edge-case disposition ("DGS2 fetch fails -> risk_free_at_expiry falls back
to the ^IRX anchor flat") is therefore not implemented or testable end-to-end.
Granted: F-2/F-3 wire pricing on the surface (D8 consumers), and the spec's F-0
test requirement explicitly lists "empty-fetch fallbacks (empty DGS2 -> ^IRX flat)"
as an F-0 deliverable test — that test does not exist. This is a mechanically
repairable missing-evidence defect within F-0's own contract, not deferred work:
the spec's disposition names risk_free_at_expiry as the fallback site.
