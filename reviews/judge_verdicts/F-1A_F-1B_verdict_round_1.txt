FINDINGS
--------
[BLOCKING — F-1A]
  test_live_contracts_same_day_roll_boundary is vacuous for its stated purpose.
  File: tests/test_leverage.py:1030-1042
  Root cause: `_make_live_contract(_T0)` creates a contract with expiry 2022-01-02;
  the roll is at _T2=2022-01-03, which is one day AFTER expiry. The `assert original
  not in live_on_roll_date` passes because the expiry guard fires, not the roll guard.
  A `<` substitution for `<=` in the rolled_out comprehension produces identical
  behavior at _T2. The test does NOT verify the <= boundary semantics it purports to test.

  Required fix: Move the roll date to a day that is WITHIN the contract's lifetime
  (i.e., roll_date <= expiry_date). The simplest fix is to use _T2 = pd.Timestamp
  ("2022-01-01") (one day before expiry) or to create a fresh contract with a later
  expiry for the same-day boundary test. The test must verify that, under the `<`
  alternative, the original WOULD be live (i.e., not excluded by any other guard).

[ADVISORY — F-1A / F-1B]

  is 2022-01-02 and _T2/_T3 are 2022-01-03/04.
  File: tests/test_leverage.py:1004-1006
  Fix: Correct the docstring or extend the contract's lifetime (e.g., purchase a
  contract with a 3-year tenor rather than the default 2-year tenor).

[ADVISORY — F-1B]
