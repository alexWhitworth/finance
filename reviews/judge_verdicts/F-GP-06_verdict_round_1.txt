  File: tests/test_glide_path_contribution.py, lines 238-247

  This test does NOT call `_apply_contribution`. It assigns `computed` and
  `expected` using identical expressions on consecutive lines, then asserts
  allclose between them. The assertion can never fail regardless of production
  code behavior. It provides zero coverage of the formula correctness inside
  _apply_contribution.

  Fix: Replace with a call that passes a known state through _apply_contribution
  and asserts the returned hurdle_contributed against the expected value, e.g.:

      state_after = _apply_contribution(state, inputs, ctx, nav_before)
      expected = old_hurdle * (1 + rfr) ** (1/12) + monthly

  correctly against _INITIAL_NAV. The tautological test is redundant AND wrong;
